    Regression models

    • Linear Regression fits a straight line by minimising squared errors.
    • Ridge and Lasso add a penalty (alpha) on large weights; Lasso can drop features.
    • SVR ignores errors inside a tube of width ε.
    • KNN, trees, Random Forest, boosting work as in Week 4, but average numbers instead of voting.

    In the explorer: choose Polynomial and move the degree from 1 to 12. Watch the training RMSE keep falling while the test RMSE rises again.

    2

    Time-series forecasting

    • Build features from the past only: lags, rolling statistics (after shift(1)), calendar.
    • Split by time: train on the past, test on the most recent period.
    • Tune with TimeSeriesSplit, never a shuffled split.
    • Always compare with naive baselines.

    3

    Evaluation and result analysis

    • MAE: typical error size, in target units.
    • RMSE: like MAE, but large errors count more.
    • R²: share of the variance explained; 0 = no better than the mean, can be negative.
    • MAPE: error as a percentage; fails when actual values are near 0.
    • Then plot residuals: a pattern means something is missing.

    4

    Challenges: overfitting, imbalance, leakage

    • Overfitting: low training error, high validation error. Regularise, simplify, add data.
    • Imbalanced data: rare classes or rare extreme values hide behind a good average score.
    • Leakage: information from the test set or the future gets into training.

    Leakage check

    For every feature, ask: would I know this value at the moment I make the prediction? A rolling mean that includes this week fails the test.

    Rare extremes

    In the lab, the overall MAE looks fine, but the error on the top 10% of weeks is more than twice as large. Always check errors on the cases that matter most.
