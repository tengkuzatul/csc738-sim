How to use a unit

  1. Work through the page from top to bottom, and tick each step in the checklist as you finish it.
  2. Try every demo: predict what will happen before you change a setting.
  3. Do the lab in Google Colab, then post your answers and reflection in i-Discuss on UFuture.

Your checklist ticks and reflections are saved only in your own browser. Post questions about the material in i-Discuss, so everyone benefits from the answers.
